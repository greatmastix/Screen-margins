How it reads numbers

  • Unit + origin say what the numbers mean on the frame: where 0,0 is, which way Y counts, and what the frame edges are worth. Use Custom range when a device has its own scale (e.g. −16…16 across).
  • Position says which point of the PiP the numbers locate: its top-left, its centre, the distance from a frame edge, or all four edges. The source sets the default; change it per PiP in the table when a document mixes formats.
  • Size is in the same units as positions, or as a scale where 1 (or 100 %) is the full frame. The lock between W and H keeps a PiP’s aspect: type whichever is given and the other follows. One size value on the target is for switchers that only take a single scale.
  • Frames of different resolution are scaled (a 4K document converts to an HD switcher). Both sides keep the same frame; nothing is cropped or letterboxed.
